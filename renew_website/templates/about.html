The RENEW Project

Intelligent System for Eco-Friendly EV Charging

The RENEW project focuses on researching and developing an intelligent energy system for eco-friendly electric vehicle charging, based on renewable energy sources.

Its goal is to optimize energy distribution and management by implementing innovations in digitalization, IoT, and predictive algorithms for energy demand.
